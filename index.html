ABOUT THE SCHOOL

QSE – PhD School is an event open to all researchers and practitioners in Software Engineering who want to explore and contribute to the quantum computing domain.

More than a traditional school, it is designed as a meeting point: a place to learn, exchange ideas, and build connections with people facing similar research challenges in Quantum Software Engineering. The focus is not only on knowledge transfer, but also on networking, discussion, and collaboration within a growing research community.

The 2027 edition takes place at the Centro Residenziale Universitario di Bertinoro (CEUB), in Bertinoro, Italy. Set in a historic hilltop complex, the centre brings teaching spaces and residential accommodation together, providing a setting for learning, exchange and informal discussions beyond the lectures.
